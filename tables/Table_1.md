M, master site and m, weaker master site (Stefanoska et al., 2022). I, site whose phosphorylation depends on prior phosphorylation at other sites in the same study. B, blood or plasma biomarker (Karikari et al., 2020; Palmqvist et al., 2020; Ashton et al., 2021). L, low conservation across vertebrates in Trushina et al. (2019). PHF, detection in paired helical filament tau by mass spectrometry (MS) or only with a phosphospecific antibody (Ab) in Hanger et al. (2007). AD and control, percentage of 49 Alzheimer disease patients and 43 controls with the site phosphorylated (Wesseling et al., 2020); a dash means the site was not reported as a single residue. Mammals identical, species of the 259 that carry the human residue; mammals S or T, species with any phosphorylatable residue at the site. Tetrapods identical, non-mammalian species that carry the human residue, over those whose best matching isoform aligns at the site, so the denominator differs between sites. At T50, for example, 2 of 225 species carry threonine and the rest isoleucine (222 sauropsids) or serine (one caecilian). FEL p tests whether the nonsynonymous rate differs from the synonymous rate at the codon.
