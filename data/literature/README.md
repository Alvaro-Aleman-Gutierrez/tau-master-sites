# Phosphorylation annotations from the literature

These files record facts transcribed from two published supplementary tables. They are not copies of the original files.

- `hanger2007_table_s10.tsv`: transcribed by hand from Table S10 of Hanger et al. (2007), J. Biol. Chem. 282, 23645–23654, https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M703269200.
  - `PHF_tau`: detection in paired helical filament tau from Alzheimer brain by mass spectrometry or Edman sequencing (`MS`), only with a phosphospecific antibody (`antibody`), or as one phosphate in a peptide without assignment to a single residue (`MS ambiguous`).
  - `control_brain`: the same, for tau from control adult brain.
  - The table lists 39 sites detected by MS (one of them either T414 or S416) and six detected only with antibodies, as stated in the article.
- `wesseling2020_table_s4_frequencies.tsv`: the rows "Frequency AD" and "Frequency CTR" of Table S4 of Wesseling et al. (2020), Cell 183, 1699–1713, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.10.029. They give the percentage of 49 Alzheimer disease patients and 43 controls with each phosphorylation in insoluble tau. Only phosphorylation entries are kept, under the names used in the original table.

Positions follow human 2N4R tau (441 residues).
